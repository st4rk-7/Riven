How the Riven extension works

Sign in at riven.sbs/app in this Chrome profile.

On a product page, click the Riven icon and choose Send this page to Riven.

Background checks: while Chrome is open, every few minutes the extension asks Riven which shared listings are due and checks them in hidden tabs, one at a time, on your internet connection. One check updates that listing for every Riven retailer tracking it. The extension gets only the listing's address, never who tracks it.

It sends only the product part of a page, never the page header or your account and address details. If a site shows a challenge page, checks on that site stop for 6 hours.

To turn background checks off, click the Riven icon and clear Check Riven's due listings in the background.
